About Me

From Words to Interfaces

My journey into tech wasn't linear. After graduating with a Bachelor's degree in Philosophy from the University of Ibadan, I realized that writing code is remarkably similar to constructing a logical argument—it requires precision, structure, and a deep understanding of human needs.

Philosophy honed my ability to think critically and approach complex problems with a unique perspective. Today, I leverage that analytical foundation to specialize in building responsive, user-centric web applications using modern tools like React, JavaScript, and CSS.
